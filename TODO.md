- Barrow: the skeleton and ghoul fights look harsh at level 1, so trim them or aim for levels 2-3. Check whether the engine can script the crown choice.
- Goblin Thief: don't use it until the licence is confirmed, in the 2021 compendium or by asking the author.
- Entries 4-6 are unread. They should be in the [2021](https://spielknights.gumroad.com/l/2021opdc), [2022](https://spielknights.gumroad.com/l/2022opdc) and [2024](https://spielknights.gumroad.com/l/2024opdc) compendia.
- Licences vary by year, so keep the notice and source URL of the exact version used, and add per-module licence and credit fields. BY-SA modules stay BY-SA, and maps adapted from an entry count too.
